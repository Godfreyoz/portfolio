About Me

The person behind the code

I'm a Full-Stack Developer and IT Specialist based in Lagos, Nigeria. At Regency Alliance Insurance Plc I own the core insurance application, the corporate website and the PostgreSQL infrastructure behind them — software that real teams depend on every working day.

I studied Computer Science at D.S. Adegbenro ICT Polytechnic and have been building for the web ever since — Django backends and REST APIs on one side, React interfaces on the other. The work I enjoy most is turning a messy real-world process into something clean enough that people stop thinking about the tool.

Outside of the day job I'm usually shipping something: wiring AI into business workflows, building real-time applications, or sharpening an interface until it feels effortless.
